## 中文常见问题

### 怎样反馈问题？

请邮件说明设备型号、系统版本、游戏版本、出现的问题和复现步骤。截图或日志为自愿提供，请先遮挡无关个人信息。不要发送密码、支付资料或不必要的完整存档。我们会用反馈资料联系你并排查该问题；可通过同一邮箱请求更正或删除。

### 花币可以提现或兑换现实奖品吗？

不可以。花币、刮卡及奖励均为游戏内的虚拟模拟内容，不提供现金兑换、提现或现实奖品。当前版本未启用实际可用的广告和应用内购买服务。

### 是否需要账号？进度能跨设备恢复吗？

当前无需注册或登录。钱包、票面刮开状态、原票收藏、经验和天赋等进度保存在设备本地，没有开发者云同步或跨设备恢复服务。卸载、清除数据或更换设备可能丢失进度。遇到存档问题时请先保留现有数据，避免反复卸载或清除；不要在没有备份的情况下尝试覆盖存档。

### 图片保存或分享为什么不可用？

游戏可生成本地 PNG 预览或文件，但平台保存方式不同。iOS 系统相册与分享桥接尚在适配中，当前不承诺相册保存或系统分享入口可用，也不代表已在 iPhone 实测。请以最终发行版本提供的功能与系统提示为准。反馈时请说明所在平台、版本和实际出现的提示。

### 谁适合游玩？

本作面向成人的漫画刮卡模拟娱乐。请遵守所在地区及最终商店页面的年龄要求；模拟结果不能作为现实购彩或获利的依据。

### How can I report an issue?

Email your device model, operating system, game version, the problem and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots and logs are optional; remove unrelated personal information first. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary complete saves. We use feedback to contact you and investigate the issue. You may request correction or deletion through the same email address.

### Can Flower Coins be cashed out or exchanged for prizes?

No. Coins, scratch cards and rewards are virtual game simulations. There is no cash redemption, withdrawal or real-world prize. The current version has no enabled functioning advertising or in-app purchase service.

### Do I need an account? Can I recover progress on another device?

No account or login is currently required. Progress, including the wallet, scratched tickets, original-ticket collection, experience and talents, is stored locally. Developer-hosted cloud sync and cross-device recovery are unavailable. Uninstalling, clearing data or changing devices may lose progress. Keep existing data when reporting a save problem; avoid repeated reinstalls, clearing data or overwriting saves without a backup.

### Why is image saving or sharing unavailable?

The game can generate local PNG previews or files, but saving differs by platform. The iOS Photos and system-sharing bridge is still being adapted. Availability is not currently promised, and this does not indicate iPhone verification. Follow the features and system prompts in the final released build. When reporting an issue, include the platform, version and exact message shown.

### Who is this game for?

It is comic scratch-card simulation entertainment intended for adults. Follow local requirements and the age rating on the final store listing. Simulated results are not guidance for real-world lottery play or profit.
