## 中文常见问题

### 怎样切换中文或英文？

支持双语的版本首次进入默认显示 English。打开设置，点击“中文”或“English”切换，选择保存在本机。中文模式保留店长教程配音，英文模式显示英语字幕。

### 怎样反馈问题？

请邮件说明设备型号、系统版本、游戏版本、实际提示和复现步骤。截图或日志可自愿提供，请先遮挡无关个人信息。购买问题可说明商品档位、大致时间和 Apple 显示的订单状态；不要发送密码、银行卡资料或不必要的完整存档。我们会用反馈资料联系你并排查问题，也可通过同一邮箱请求更正或删除。

### 金条、花币和奖励可以转让或兑换现金吗？

不可以。它们只能用于本作，不能转让、提现或兑换现金、实物及现实奖品。购买的虚拟币不设到期日，但余额和使用记录保存在本机；卸载、清除数据或丢失设备仍可能使其无法恢复。

### 金条如何购买和使用？

提供内购的 iOS 版本通过 Apple 购买金条。小铺有 50、160、280、600、1300 金条五档，价格以 Apple 的购买确认页为准。每档在当前本地进度中的首次成功购买会赠送等量金条；该记录不跨设备同步。

花币不足时，游戏会先显示需要补足的差额。经你确认后，按 1 金条补充 10 花币使用，所需金条向上取整，剩余花币保留。取消不会扣币。这只是游戏内补差方式，不是现金兑换。游戏没有广告充值服务。

### Apple 显示已付款，金条却未到账怎么办？

先保留现有游戏数据，确认网络可用后重开游戏，让尚未完成的 Apple 订单重试处理。游戏只对验证通过的交易发币，同笔已记账订单不会重复发放；取消、等待批准或未通过验证时不会发币。仍有问题请联系上述支持邮箱。

请勿为排查而立即卸载或清除数据。消耗型金条购买不能用来恢复已消费的金条、完整本地钱包或另一设备的首购记录；Apple 的订单记录不是完整游戏存档。支付与退款由 Apple 按其流程处理。

### 是否需要账号？更换设备后能恢复进度吗？

不需要游戏账号。钱包、已购票及刮痕、原票收藏、经验和天赋等保存在设备本地，没有开发者云存档或跨设备同步服务。每档首购倍赠也按本地进度记录。系统备份或迁移是否保留数据取决于系统与所选服务，本作不保证完整恢复。遇到存档问题时先保留现有数据，不要反复卸载、清除或覆盖存档。

### 怎样保存或分享原票图片？

在提供此功能的 iOS 版本中，原票先在本机生成 PNG 预览。主动打开系统分享面板后，可选择“存储到文件”或接收图片的应用；可用目标以设备系统和已安装应用为准。

此版不提供直接存入照片相册的选项，也不申请相册、相机或麦克风权限。系统报告分享操作完成，不代表已经在社交平台真实发帖。开发者不会收到所分享的图片或完整存档。0.1.7 仅支持应用内预览，没有 iOS 系统分享。

### 谁适合游玩？

本作面向成人的漫画刮卡模拟娱乐。请遵守所在地要求及商店年龄分级；模拟结果不能作为现实购彩或获利依据。

### How can I report an issue?

Email your device model, operating system, game version, exact message and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots and logs are optional; remove unrelated personal information first. For purchase issues, you may describe the product tier, approximate time and order status shown by Apple. Do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary complete saves. We use feedback to contact you and investigate the issue. You may request correction or deletion through the same email address.

### Can Gold Bars, Flower Coins or rewards be transferred or redeemed for money?

No. They are for use in this game only and cannot be transferred, cashed out or exchanged for money, physical goods or real-world prizes. Purchased virtual currency has no expiry date, but balances and usage are stored locally. Uninstalling, clearing data or losing the device may still make them unrecoverable.

### How do I buy and use Gold Bars?

iOS versions offering purchases use Apple for Gold Bar payments. The shop has tiers of 50, 160, 280, 600 and 1300 Gold Bars. Follow the price in Apple's purchase confirmation. The first successful purchase of each tier in your current local progress grants an equal number of bonus Gold Bars. This record does not sync across devices.

When Flower Coins are insufficient, the game first shows the shortfall. After you confirm, each Gold Bar adds 10 Flower Coins toward the purchase. Required Gold Bars are rounded up, and any remaining Flower Coins stay in your wallet. Cancelling spends nothing. This is an in-game shortfall conversion, not a money exchange. The game does not offer advertising-based top-ups.

### Apple shows a charge, but my Gold Bars are missing. What should I do?

Keep your existing game data, check your connection and reopen the game so unfinished Apple transactions can be retried. Only verified transactions award currency. A transaction already recorded locally is not credited again; cancelled, pending or unverified transactions award nothing. If the issue remains, contact the support email above.

Do not immediately reinstall or clear data to troubleshoot. Consumable Gold Bar purchases cannot restore spent Gold Bars, your complete local wallet or first-purchase records on another device. Apple's order history is not a complete game save. Apple handles payment and refund requests through its own processes.

### Do I need an account? Can progress be recovered on another device?

No game account is required. Wallets, purchased tickets and scratch marks, original-ticket collections, experience and talents are stored locally, with no developer-hosted cloud saves or cross-device sync. First-purchase bonuses are also recorded locally. Whether a system backup or transfer preserves data depends on the operating system and services you choose; we do not guarantee full recovery. Keep existing data when reporting a save problem, and avoid repeated reinstalls, clearing data or overwriting saves.

### How can I save or share an original-ticket image?

In iOS versions offering this feature, the game first generates a PNG preview on your device. You can then choose to open the system share sheet and select Save to Files or an app that accepts the image. Available destinations depend on the system and installed apps.

This version does not offer direct saving to Photos and does not request Photos, camera or microphone permission. A system completion callback does not prove that the image was actually posted on a social platform. The developer does not receive the shared image or your complete save. Version 0.1.7 offers an in-app preview only, without iOS system sharing.

### Who is this game for?

This is comic scratch-card simulation entertainment intended for adults. Follow local requirements and the store age rating. Simulated results are not guidance for real-world lottery play or profit.
