## 中文

### 1. 游戏与本地进度

《漫画刮刮乐》是使用虚拟花币的漫画刮卡模拟游戏。花币和游戏奖励不能兑换现金、提现或领取现实奖品。当前版本无需注册或登录，未启用云同步、统计分析、实际可用的广告或应用内购买服务。

游戏将钱包、已购票及刮开状态、原票收藏、经验、等级、天赋、签到和设置等进度保存在本机应用数据中，以便继续游玩。游戏不会主动将完整存档上传到开发者服务器。系统备份或设备迁移可能由设备系统及玩家选择的服务处理。

卸载游戏、清除应用数据或丢失设备可能导致本地进度无法恢复。当前没有开发者云端存档恢复或跨设备同步服务。

### 2. 设备运行信息与权限

Godot 引擎在设备本地使用系统、屏幕、输入、图形及音频等运行信息，以提供渲染、声音和兼容性支持。当前版本未启用用于跟踪玩家或上传分析数据的 SDK。设备系统及应用商店可能依据其自身设置和政策处理备份或诊断资料。

普通游玩不需要提供通讯录、精确位置、相机或麦克风信息。具体权限以最终发行包和系统提示为准；我们不会把可选图片保存权限作为继续普通游玩的条件。

### 3. 图片预览、保存与分享

游戏可在本地生成卡票 PNG 预览或文件。iOS 系统相册与分享桥接仍在适配中，不代表当前已提供或已在 iPhone 验证；最终可用入口及权限以发行版本为准。

如果发行版本提供系统保存或分享入口，玩家主动使用时，设备系统或玩家选择的服务会处理所选图片，并适用其自身政策。此操作不会使游戏自动上传完整存档。分享前请检查图片中是否包含不希望他人看到的内容。

### 4. 主动反馈与删除请求

通过邮件反馈时，我们会收到玩家自愿提供的邮箱、问题说明，以及自行附加的截图、设备资料或日志。这些资料仅用于联系、排查和解决所反馈的问题，并仅在处理所需期间保留。请遮挡无关个人信息，不要发送密码、支付资料或不必要的完整存档。

玩家可以通过上述邮箱请求查询、更正或删除反馈资料；我们会核实请求并按适用要求处理，依法必须保留的资料除外。本地游戏数据可通过设备系统清除应用数据或卸载应用删除，操作前请注意进度丢失风险。

### 5. 年龄与政策更新

本作面向成人的模拟刮卡娱乐，不以儿童为受众，也不需要收集儿童个人信息。请遵守所在地区及最终商店页面的年龄要求。如发现儿童在反馈中提供了不必要的个人信息，监护人可联系我们处理。

在正式启用广告、支付、统计、云存档、第三方登录或其他改变信息处理方式的 SDK／服务前，我们会更新本政策，并在需要时提供提示或取得同意。

### 1. The game and local progress

《漫画刮刮乐》 is a comic scratch-card simulation using virtual Flower Coins. Coins and rewards have no cash-out, real-money redemption or real-world prize value. The current version requires no account or login and has no enabled cloud sync, analytics, functioning advertising or in-app purchase service.

The game stores progress in the app's local device storage, including the wallet, purchased tickets and scratch state, original-ticket collection, experience, levels, talents, check-ins and settings. It does not proactively upload complete saves to a developer server. Device backups or transfers may be handled by the operating system or services you choose.

Uninstalling the game, clearing its data or losing the device may permanently remove progress. Developer-hosted save recovery and cross-device sync are not currently available.

### 2. Device operation and permissions

The Godot engine uses local system, display, input, graphics and audio information to run rendering, sound and compatibility functions. The current version has no enabled SDK for tracking players or uploading analytics. Your operating system or app store may process backups or diagnostic information under its own settings and policies.

Ordinary play does not require contacts, precise location, camera or microphone information. Actual permissions depend on the final build and system prompts. Optional image-saving permission is not a condition of ordinary play.

### 3. Image previews, saving and sharing

The game can generate ticket PNG previews or files locally. The iOS Photos and system-sharing bridge is still being adapted; this does not mean it is currently available or verified on an iPhone. Available actions and permissions depend on the released build.

If a released build offers system saving or sharing, using it voluntarily lets the operating system or your selected service process the chosen image under its own policies. This does not cause the game to upload your complete save automatically. Review images before sharing anything you wish to keep private.

### 4. Voluntary feedback and deletion requests

When you email support, we receive the email address, issue description and any screenshots, device details or logs you voluntarily provide. We use them only to contact you and investigate and resolve the reported issue, retaining them only as long as necessary for that purpose. Remove unrelated personal information and do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary complete saves.

You may email us to request access to, correction of or deletion of feedback information. We will verify and handle requests as applicable, except for information that must legally be retained. You can delete local game data through the device's clear-data or uninstall controls; this may permanently remove progress.

### 5. Age and policy changes

This is simulated scratch-card entertainment intended for adults. It is not directed at children and does not need children's personal information. Follow local requirements and the age rating shown on the final store listing. A guardian may contact us if a child supplies unnecessary personal information in feedback.

Before enabling advertising, payments, analytics, cloud saves, third-party login or another SDK/service that changes information handling, we will update this policy and provide notice or obtain consent where needed.
