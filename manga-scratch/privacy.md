### 1. 游戏与本地进度

《漫画刮刮乐》是漫画刮卡模拟游戏。金条、花币和游戏奖励只能在本作内使用，不能转让、提现，或兑换现金、实物及现实奖品。

游戏无需注册或登录，没有开发者云存档、统计分析、广告或追踪服务。钱包、已购票及刮开状态、原票收藏、经验、天赋、签到和设置等进度保存在本机。游戏不把完整存档上传给开发者，也未连接开发者自建服务器。

卸载游戏、清除应用数据或丢失设备可能使本地进度无法恢复，包括未使用的付费金条和每档首购记录。系统备份或设备迁移由设备系统及你选择的服务处理，本作不保证完整钱包或跨设备进度恢复。购买的虚拟币不设到期日，但这不等于云端保管或丢失后可恢复。

### 2. Apple 内购与本地订单记录

提供内购的 iOS 版本使用 Apple StoreKit 处理金条购买。付款确认与支付资料由 Apple 处理，开发者不会收到你的银行卡号或银行账户资料。Apple 如何处理账户、购买及付款信息，请参阅 [App Store 与隐私](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/)。

游戏在设备上核验 Apple 返回的交易结果，只有验证通过后才记录到账。本地保存交易编号、商品标识、正式或测试商店环境、金条余额及使用记录、每档首购状态，用于发放金条、防止同笔订单重复到账和重试未完成订单。这些本地记录不发送到开发者服务器。取消、等待批准或未通过验证的交易不会发币。

每档首次购买的双倍赠送按本机进度记录。已完成的消耗型购买不等于一份可恢复的完整钱包；本作不承诺恢复已消费的金条、完整本地余额或跨设备首购状态。详情见[技术支持](support.md)。

### 3. 设备运行信息与权限

Godot 引擎在设备本地使用系统、屏幕、输入、图形及音频信息，以运行游戏。本作没有上传使用分析或追踪玩家的 SDK。设备系统与应用商店可能按各自设置和政策处理备份、诊断及商店服务信息。

本 iOS 版本不请求相册、相机或麦克风权限，也不需要通讯录或精确位置。播放游戏声音不需要麦克风访问。

### 4. PNG 预览与主动分享

游戏在本机生成原票 PNG。你主动打开系统分享面板后，可选择系统提供的“存储到文件”或接收图片的应用。开发者不会因此收到图片或完整存档。

本 iOS 版本不提供直接存入照片相册的选项。接收应用、文件位置和后续操作由你选择，并适用所选服务的政策。系统报告分享操作完成，不证明图片已经真实发布到社交平台。分享前请检查图片内容。

你向支持邮箱发信时，我们会收到你提供的邮箱、问题说明及自愿附加的截图、设备资料或日志。我们仅用这些资料联系你、排查和解决反馈问题，并只在处理所需期间保留。请遮挡无关个人信息，不要发送密码、银行卡资料或不必要的完整存档。

### 1. The game and local progress

《漫画刮刮乐》 is a comic scratch-card simulation. Gold Bars, Flower Coins and rewards are for use within this game only. They cannot be transferred, cashed out or exchanged for money, physical goods or real-world prizes.

No registration or game account is required. The game has no developer-hosted cloud saves, analytics, advertising or tracking service. Progress—including wallets, purchased tickets and scratch state, original-ticket collections, experience, talents, check-ins and settings—is stored on your device. The game does not upload complete saves to the developer and has no developer-operated server connection.

Uninstalling, clearing app data or losing your device may permanently remove progress, including unused paid Gold Bars and first-purchase records. Device backups or transfers are handled by the operating system or services you choose. We do not guarantee recovery of your complete wallet or progress on another device. Purchased virtual currency has no expiry date; this does not provide cloud storage or guarantee recovery after data loss.

### 2. Apple purchases and local transaction records

iOS versions offering purchases use Apple StoreKit for Gold Bars. Apple handles payment confirmation and payment information. The developer does not receive your payment card number or bank account details. See [App Store & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/) for Apple's handling of account, purchase and payment information.

The game verifies Apple's transaction results on the device before crediting Gold Bars. It stores transaction IDs, product identifiers, the production or test store environment, Gold Bar balances and usage, and first-purchase status locally. These records support delivery, prevent duplicate credits for the same transaction and allow unfinished transactions to be retried. They are not sent to a developer server. Cancelled, pending or unverified transactions do not award currency.

The double bonus for each tier's first purchase is recorded in local progress. A completed consumable purchase is not a backup of your full wallet. We do not promise recovery of spent Gold Bars, the complete local balance or first-purchase status on another device. See [Support](support.md).

### 3. Device operation and permissions

The Godot engine uses system, display, input, graphics and audio information locally to run the game. No SDK uploads usage analytics or tracks players. The operating system and app store may handle backups, diagnostics and store-service information under their own settings and policies.

This iOS version does not request Photos, camera or microphone permission, and does not need contacts or precise location. Playing game audio does not require microphone access.

### 4. PNG previews and sharing you choose

The game generates original-ticket PNGs locally. When you choose to open the system share sheet, you can select Save to Files, where offered by the system, or an app that accepts the image. This does not send the image or your complete save to the developer.

This iOS version does not offer direct saving to Photos. You choose the receiving app, file destination and any further action, which are subject to the selected service's policies. A system completion callback does not prove that an image was actually published on a social platform. Review the image before sharing.

### 5. Voluntary feedback and deletion requests

When you email support, we receive the email address, issue description and any screenshots, device information or logs you choose to provide. We use this information only to contact you and investigate and resolve the issue, retaining it only as long as needed for that purpose. Remove unrelated personal information. Do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary complete saves.

You may use the contact email above to request access to, correction of or deletion of feedback information. We will verify and handle requests as applicable, except where retention is legally required. Clearing app data or uninstalling can delete local data, including unused paid currency, permanently. Use these controls carefully.

### 6. Age and policy updates

This is simulated scratch-card entertainment intended for adults, not children. Follow local requirements and the store age rating. Simulated results are not guidance for real-world lottery play or profit. A guardian may contact us about unnecessary personal information supplied by a child in feedback.

If we later add advertising, analytics, cloud saves, third-party login or another change to information handling, we will update this policy and provide notice or obtain consent where required.
